The Sandbells Sandmon project.

**What?**
An attempt to calibrate the St Mary's Sandbach's Tower Clock.

**Why?**
I'll get back to you on that one, but it's got a lot to do with accuracy and time keeping.

**How?**
A Raspberry Pi zero 2W, 
some hastily contrived Veroboard, 
a GPS Receiver, 
A Buck Converter, 
an LCD Display,
a OneWire Temperature Sensor
and  a RGB 12c Encoder,

& Finally . . .

a USB Microphone. To listen to the clock.

All in a little plastic Box.
With another one-wire sensor hanging out the side to measure clock room temperature.
